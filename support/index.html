Questions, problems or ideas: write to daystone@linesoftyazilim.com. Every message is read.

Where is my garden kept?
Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server.
How do I keep a copy?
Settings › Your data › Export my data saves a file. Your iPhone's own backup (iCloud or a computer) also includes your garden.
What happens when I change phones?
Set up the new iPhone from a backup of the old one, or use Settings › Your data › Restore from a file with an exported file.
The reminder doesn't arrive.
Check Settings › Reminder in Daystone, and that notifications are allowed for Daystone in the iPhone's Settings › Notifications. No reminder comes on a day you've already touched.
How do I delete everything?
Settings › Your data › Delete all data. This can't be undone.

Daystone needs iOS 26 or later.
