Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

The short version

Daystone collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not connect to the internet.

Your garden stays on your iPhone

Your days, words, notes and monthly readings are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. They are part of your iPhone's own backup (iCloud or a computer), the way iOS backs up any app. Daystone never sends them anywhere.

Reminders and lock

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. If you turn on the Face ID lock, iOS checks your face; Daystone never sees it.

Exporting your data

Settings › Your data › Export my data creates a file that you choose where to save or share. It holds your days, words and notes in plain text (JSON), including notes that stay hidden in the app. Keep it somewhere you trust.

Deleting your data

Settings › Your data › Delete all data removes everything from your iPhone. Deleting the app does the same.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new version appears on this page.
